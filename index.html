Welcome to Chestnut House, an impressive five bedroom detached family home set on approximately one-third of an acre of beautifully maintained grounds, offering an exceptional blend of privacy, space and convenience.

Approached through secure wrought-iron gates, the property immediately impresses with its attractive façade, mature landscaping and extensive cobblelock and gravel driveway providing ample off-street parking for numerous vehicles. The stylish entrance porch and well-maintained grounds create a wonderful first impression and reflect the high standard of care evident throughout the home.

Internally, Chestnut House offers gracious and well proportioned accommodation designed with modern family living in mind. At the heart of the home is a superb bespoke solid wood kitchen featuring a large central island, extensive storage, quality integrated appliances and attractive timber flooring, all complemented by a large overhead rooflight which floods the space with natural light.

The solid wood kitchen is perfectly positioned for both everyday family life and entertaining guests. The elegant living room is a standout feature of the home, boasting high ceilings, a feature fireplace with open fire, attractive timber flooring and large sliding patio doors that frame views of the mature gardens while allowing natural light to pour into the room.

The generously proportioned reception spaces create a wonderful environment for both everyday family living and entertaining guests.

The accommodation extends to five spacious bedrooms, with the principal bedroom suite providing a walk-in wardrobe and private en-suite bathroom, creating a luxurious retreat within the home. The remaining bedrooms are well proportioned and are served by a stylish family bathroom.

Outside, the generous site offers excellent privacy and ample space for family living and recreation. A particular feature is the substantial detached shed/workshop to the rear, providing excellent versatility and suitability for storage, hobbies, a home gym, workshop or home office.

The outbuilding offers scope to explore a small, separate habitable home for visiting guests or family, permissible without planning permission (under Ireland's new regulations introduced in July 2026)

Combining spacious accommodation, mature, landscaped grounds, extensive parking and a versatile, detached outbuilding, Chestnut House presents a rare opportunity to acquire an exceptional family home in a peaceful setting.

Chestnut House enjoys a peaceful and highly sought-after setting while remaining within easy reach of Swords and a host of local amenities. Swords Village is just a short drive away and offers an excellent selection of shops, schools, cafés, restaurants and sporting facilities. The award-winning Roganstown Golf & Country Club is also nearby, providing an 18-hole championship golf course along with excellent leisure and fitness facilities. The M1 and M50 motorways are easily accessible, offering convenient access to Dublin Airport, Dublin City Centre and the wider road network. Regular public transport services operate nearby, providing straightforward connectivity to Swords, Ashbourne and Dublin City Centre.

Viewing is highly recommended to fully appreciate all that this wonderful home has to offer.
